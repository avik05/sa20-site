    01
    Chapter oneKolkata · 2022 — 2024

    The plan we ignored

    A B.Com at Heritage, a first trip to Darjeeling, and a CAT course that sat unopened for ten months.

    2022 → Oct 2024

    Friends first. Coaching second. Studying, eventually.

    We became friends at Heritage College in 2022. In September 2023 we went to Darjeeling together, which set a pattern: most of the important things in this story happen on a hill.

    In October 2024 we bought the Rodha course for CAT 2025. Then Calcutta University's semester exams took over. We watched a few lectures, admired how good they were, and forgot the whole thing existed until August.

    02
    Chapter twoKolkata · Aug — Dec 2025

    The Sheet

    No job offers, a huge backlog and one Excel file that ran our lives for four months.

    August 2025

    "We were like a robot."

    College ended and neither of us had a job. An MBA was the bet, and only a good school would make it worth anything. Avik took a backup: M.Com at St. Xavier's, Kolkata. He lasted one week before deciding he couldn't do both.

    The real work started the day after Mahesh's birthday, 25 July. Avik built an Excel sheet with a tab for everything: lectures, topic tests, mocks, month by month. Arithmetic first for Quant, LR first for LRDI. Wake up, sit down, watch, solve, eat, repeat.

    Note

    All we ever finished in Quant was arithmetic. It turned out to be most of the paper.

    30 Nov 2025 · Exam day

    Two papers, two different rescues.

    Avik

    95 percentile. Carried by VARC and LRDI.

    Quant sectional wasn't pretty. The other two sections did the lifting. The result landed the day before his first ever MBA interview.

    Mahesh

    Scoring 5 or 6 in LRDI mocks. Then 35 on the day.

    VARC, his banker, suddenly felt impossible. LRDI saved him: two sets solved clean, a third guessed outright, on type-in answers, and every guess was right. 99.66 percentile in the section.

    R0

    The round we applied in, by accident. There were four.

    25 & 28 Dec 2025

    The interview, passed forward.

    Avik went first, on Christmas Day. Twenty minutes, first interview of his life, "pretty average". He was asked the founder's name and fumbled it. Afterwards he told Mahesh he'd happily take the Gurgaon campus if it came to that.

    Mahesh went three days later with Avik's notes: expect guesstimates, and learn the founder. He walked in knowing everything about Bala V. Balachandran, then estimated how many people visit malls in Kolkata per day, and walked out with no idea what would happen next.

    03
    Chapter threeBara Mangwa, near Darjeeling · 16 — 19 Jan 2026

    7,000 ft

    Six friends, an overnight train nobody slept on, a river nobody planned for, and one email.

    16 Jan · Morning

    Three for, three against.

    Mahesh, Avik, Srijit, Shubham, Aditya and Dhruv took the night train to Siliguri. Nobody slept. Instead of checking in, we went rafting on the Teesta with our bags still in the car, without telling anyone.

    There was a short route and a 10 km one with real waves. Srijit, Dhruv and Shubham wanted the long one. Mahesh, Avik and Aditya pointed out that it could kill us. The argument that won: we're only doing this once.

    On the drive there the two of us looked at each other in complete silence while the back seat chanted the Hanuman Chalisa. Nobody fell in. The ride back, soaked through in January, was the cold part.

    16 Jan · Night

    Old Monk, and one phone call that didn't help.

    We celebrated with Old Monk. It was the first drink of Avik's life. His parents told him they were proud and to enjoy the mountains.

    Mahesh called his father about the fees, and then his sister called to say Great Lakes wasn't a good college and he shouldn't go.

    "You finally get a college, and your sister calls to tell you it's not worth it. Doesn't matter. I'm here now."

    Mahesh
    17 — 19 Jan

    The rest of the hill.

    17 Jan: Lamahatta, Kalimpong and the Art Cafe. 18 Jan: the orange garden and factory, then a two-hour trek to Chota Mangwa with a gentle guide, a pack of mountain dogs, and a stranger who fell off his bike in front of us (Srijit helped lift it; there is video). Aditya was ill and stayed back. 19 Jan: home.

    Around all this, Mahesh finished and released his eighth album, Rivers Apart, in mid-January. Fourteen tracks, just over an hour.

    Chapter fourMumbai & Kolkata · Feb 2026

    The interview circuit

    With one admit in hand, every other interview became optional. We went anyway.

    8 — 13 Feb · Mumbai

    An interview trip that turned into a holiday.

    Up at 4 AM for a 7 AM flight. By afternoon: Juhu beach, Prithvi Cafe, Bandra Fort. Mahesh had a Belgian torte from Theobroma, Avik did not, and then Mahesh slept from 10:30 PM to 10:30 AM.

    The NMIMS interviews on 9 February went fine. Without work experience there were no points to be had, so neither of us was selected, and the rest was Gateway of India and Marine Drive.

    05
    Chapter fiveKolkata · Mar — Jun 2026

    The last free months

    Video games, biryani, one storm, and the week we found out we could build things.

    March — April

    "It did change our lives."

    Avik started an AI-for-finance course, began using Claude, and installed Antigravity purely because its website looked nice. He asked it for a mock finance dashboard to see what it could do. Mahesh followed a few days later. Each built what he loves.

    Avik built

    Finkar. Four sites in about fifteen days.

    Dashboard, news, games and tools, for MBA students and anyone who reads finance news. Full stack, own database, secured, live on its own domain.

    Mahesh built

    A site on 4 April. A music theory app by the 10th.

    Domain from Namecheap, ₹700 for the year. Then the rabbit hole: Ollama, Open WebUI and local models on the new Acer Nitro V15, because tokens run out.

    Also in this period

    Things Mahesh did before the MBA took his time away.

    Played everything he'd missed in ten years without games: Hades, Red Dead Redemption 2, Overcooked 2. Rewatched Hunter × Hunter. Started a novel, The Chromic Path, and abandoned it at 20,000 words.

    On 18 May he made a ninth album and called it One Last Time, because he assumed there would be no time for another for two years. That assumption lasted three months.

    Mahesh

    "I could not write, because I have never read a book in my life."

    7 — 8 May · Avik's birthday

    The storm, the rum and the burger that never came.

    Mahesh and Srijit stayed the night. The plan needed Old Monk, so we went out for it, and a storm arrived at the same time. We came back drenched with the bottle. Avik had ordered a burger he'd promised Mahesh would love. It never showed up. We ate chicken wings.

    Next day we rode around Kolkata on bike and scooty for what felt like the last time, and Avik took us to Trincas on Park Street for chicken à la Kiev.

    The present: a signed Lando Norris photo frame and a McLaren diecast.

    Chapter sixKolkata → Chennai · 23 Jun 2026

    Request denied

    We asked Great Lakes to room us together. They said it wasn't possible until second year.

    Two hours before the flight, the room allocation email arrived. My roommate was Mahesh Arora.

    Avik
    23 Jun · Departure

    Nobody knows who did it.

    Avik

    Called immediately, screaming.

    "We still don't know if it was a coincidence or someone did it in the back end. Whatever it is, it changed how we do everything here."

    Mahesh

    Was in a car with his family.

    Said "okay, damn, we are roommates, damn" with no emotion whatsoever. Was, internally, losing his mind.

    Same flight, both sets of parents. A storm hit Kolkata exactly at take-off. Two and a half hours later a student-run cab service drove us to Mahabalipuram, where half the incoming batch was staying at the same hotel.

    24 Jun · Arrival

    Saraswati hostel, ground floor, room SA 20.

    Keys at the gate around 8 AM. Saraswati is the new hostel, built after learning from everything wrong with the old one, so it feels like a hotel. The room isn't big. It has AC, it's polished, and it's ours.

    Registration at LMLV Hall took two hours. Then a first lap of campus, ending at the Buddha statue Avik had only ever seen on the website.

    We were exhausted and nearly went to sleep. Instead we went out looking for people. Neither of us had lived in a hostel before.

    Chapter nineGreat Lakes · Sep 2026

    Ten minutes left

    The first company on campus, a last-minute application, and the most hectic ten days of the term.

    Avik · Mastercard

    Applied for the McLaren logo. Reached the final thirty.

    Mastercard came for product and pricing strategy. Neither fit, so we weren't applying. With twenty minutes to go, Avik realised most of the batch didn't meet the 75% cut-off and he did. He submitted with ten minutes left. Mahesh tried too, and Skynet glitched out on him.

    —
    HR round: not shortlisted

    He learned pricing strategy in three hours for the case. On the day, the room went silent for five seconds when the GD opened, and the person who never starts a group discussion started it, with a framework. GD done by 11 AM. Interview at 5 PM. Twenty-five calm minutes and a finance case. Two more hours of waiting, then the list.

    Avik

    "Never in my life would I be the person to start a group discussion. I don't know how. I just started speaking."

    Mahesh · Brand Conclave

    The one time BPR was fun.

    Running operations and scoring for Brand Feud, with a custom ID card, hosting duties and the authority to disqualify a team for cheating, which he used. Afterwards the faculty in charge threatened to fire most of the committee. Mahesh's reaction was "finally". As the person holding the budget and the socials, he remains employed.

    Mahesh · End-terms

    The wrong email.

    FADM exam in the morning after three or four hours of sleep. Then four straight hours of pre-placement sessions for two companies. He saw a Skynet mail, believed he was shortlisted, and relaxed.

    At 10 PM he read it properly. It had gone to everyone. He was not shortlisted, and Marketing and Economics were both the next morning. He skimmed two subjects overnight and sat them.

    22 September. Operations and Statistics on the same day. Then suits on in two minutes for a JP Morgan talk.
    22 Sep · Night

    How term one ended.

    Pizza, shawarma, noodles and chicken lollipop. Both mattresses dragged onto the floor, a laptop between them, a show playing. We finished eating and were asleep almost before the plates were down.

    "Even when I woke up, I was like: where am I?"

    Mahesh
